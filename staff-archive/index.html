2019 All-Hands Recap

Posted October 2019 · Company Blog

We closed out the office for an afternoon this month and got the whole team together for our first all-hands in almost a year. Good food, a few overdue conversations about where Fernbrook is headed next, and a group photo for posterity (see below, and no, nobody is allowed to crop themselves out).

L to R: R. Alvarez (Ops), K. Tran (Admin), Steve B. (IT), M. Ilyin (Support), E. Riley (Office)

A few highlights from the afternoon: we're up to eleven people now (wild, considering we started this thing out of a garage), the coffee machine finally got replaced, and IT spent a solid ten minutes trying to convince everyone that "have you tried turning it off and on again" is, statistically, correct more often than not.
