About Fernbrook

Started by three people who were tired of bad IT vendors.

Fernbrook Systems opened its doors in 2015 as a two-person outfit answering support calls out of a converted garage. A decade later, we're still small on purpose, just better equipped.

We work with local businesses who've outgrown "the nephew who's good with computers" but aren't ready for a full internal IT department. That's most of our client base: medical offices, contractors, small law firms, a couple of nonprofits, and a growing list of remote-friendly teams who need someone to own their network without being on payroll.

Our approach hasn't changed much since the garage days: answer the phone, show up when we say we will, and fix the actual problem instead of the symptom. We've picked up a few industry recognitions along the way, which we're admittedly a little proud of.

Today the team handles managed IT, helpdesk support, and network security for clients across the region. Want the specifics on who you'd actually be working with? Meet the team.
